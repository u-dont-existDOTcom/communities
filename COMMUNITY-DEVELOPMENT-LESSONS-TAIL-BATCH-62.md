# Community development lessons — embedded seed experiments

Date: 2026-08-16
Disposition: practical design lesson; not a new empirical finding

## Core architecture

Institutional experimentation should not normally require a group to leave the parent community first. Where the proposed variation is compatible with continued co-residence or close adjacency, allow a small seed experiment to operate inside the community or immediately beside it while continuing to share selected infrastructure, relationships, services, and resources.

Treat full daughter-community formation as an optional graduation path, not the starting condition. A seed may instead remain nested, be absorbed by the parent, diffuse one or more successful practices, or separate only when incompatibility, scale, land, autonomy, or interface costs make separation worthwhile.

Do not make a large irreversible resource transfer merely because a faction proposes an attractive theory. Use staged evidence and increasing commitments where feasible. The practical analogy supplied by the user: do not provide $10,000 to buy a dinosaur on the promise that it will work; first show the dinosaur and show that it can be ridden safely, then consider the larger commitment.

## Important limits

1. **Proofability bias.** Some variants can be demonstrated cheaply inside the parent; others require a complementary package, scale, land, or autonomy before their value can become visible. A proof-first rule can therefore reproduce the existing C003 asset-gated cultural-mutation problem if only cheap, incumbent-compatible variants can reach the proof stage.
2. **Shared-resource accounting.** Do not confuse value while supported by parent infrastructure with autonomous viability. But also do not require autonomy when the intended mature arrangement is a federation with durable shared services; evaluate against the actual intended resource model.
3. **Graduation hold-up.** If seed members invest substantial labor before the parent decides ex post whether demonstrated success earns further resources or autonomy, specify important graduation criteria/resource options early enough to avoid an avoidable bargaining bottleneck, subject to safety and fiduciary overrides.
4. **Interface learning.** An adjacent partially autonomous seed can expose the real boundary problems a later federation will face: shared land and tools, money, childcare, records, dispute jurisdiction, labor exchange, family ties, and other cross-unit dependencies.

## Relation to existing research

The completed corpus already supports planned fission, translocal federation, role-specific governance, reversible trials, and C003 asset-gated cultural mutation. Hutterite daughter-colony practice supplies a nearby empirical analogue for preparing a daughter site before the final split, but not for deliberately incubating divergent institutional experiments. This lesson should therefore remain an operational synthesis rather than a promoted evidence claim.
