# Main lesson — Separate empirical research from federation-level value aggregation

**Status:** FOUNDATIONAL PRACTICAL / KNOWN + RESEARCH ARCHITECTURE

A federation research center can compare facts only where those facts/constructs are genuinely comparable. Even perfect measurement does **not** create one neutral definition of which community or practice is `better` when communities value the outcomes differently.

Therefore the research center should not quietly become the federation's philosopher-king by embedding its own weights in a success score.

Recommended mission:

> **Estimate what tends to cause what, under which conditions, with what uncertainty and tradeoffs. Let communities apply their own values unless the federation has explicitly adopted a common constitutional floor or aggregation rule.**

---

## B39-L01 — Treat measurement comparability and value comparability as separate gates

**Status:** FOUNDATIONAL

Before any federation-level comparison ask two different questions:

1. **Measurement gate:** are the constructs/outcomes commensurate enough to compare?  
   Use C022 measurement-canary and ordinary validity/invariance methods where needed.
2. **Valuation gate:** even if the outcomes are comparable, is there a legitimate shared rule for deciding which outcomes matter more and what tradeoffs are acceptable?

Passing the first gate does not imply the second.

## B39-L02 — Use a three-layer research-center architecture

### Layer 1 — empirical outcomes

Estimate outcomes and causal effects without treating them as an automatic ranking.

Examples can include injury, mortality, ecological impact, resource use, exit time/liquidity, child outcomes, member-reported autonomy, conflict, relationship stability, reproduction, retention and leaver outcomes.

### Layer 2 — community valuation

Allow each community to declare its own:
- goals;
- floors/thresholds;
- non-compensatory constraints;
- tradeoffs/weights where genuinely used;
- outcomes it refuses to collapse into a scalar score.

The research center can then issue community-conditional recommendations from the same evidence.

### Layer 3 — federation constitutional floor

The federation may explicitly adopt shared rights, safeguarding duties, data-integrity requirements, or other common constraints.

Those are governance/constitutional choices, **not empirical discoveries by the research center**.

## B39-L03 — Do not publish a federation leaderboard without an explicit normative constitution

**Status:** FOUNDATIONAL

Any single `community quality` or `best community` number necessarily selects:
- which outcomes count;
- normalization/scales;
- weights/tradeoffs;
- whether one benefit can offset another harm;
- whose preferences matter;
- how distribution within the community is treated;
- which outcomes are floors versus compensable dimensions.

If the federation wants such a score, the weighting/aggregation rule should be explicit, contestable and governed. It should not masquerade as neutral science.

## B39-L04 — Prefer causal/tradeoff maps to universal recommendations

**Status:** PRACTICAL / KNOWN

The center's high-value product is often:

- intervention X tends to increase A;
- it tends to reduce B;
- effect depends on conditions C/D;
- uncertainty is E;
- subgroup/child/leaver outcomes differ;
- transportability is limited in these settings.

A community can then apply its own value structure to the tradeoff.

## B39-L05 — Use partial ordering when no legitimate common weighting exists

**Status:** PRACTICAL / KNOWN

Do not force a total ranking when evidence only supports a partial one.

Useful outputs:
- dominated options;
- non-dominated/Pareto frontier;
- explicit tradeoff table;
- value-profile sensitivity;
- `not rankable without a normative judgment`.

The last output is a valid research conclusion, not a research failure.

## B39-L06 — Give every metric a comparison jurisdiction

**Status:** PRACTICAL / KNOWN

Tag the valid scope of each measure:

1. within-person/household over time;
2. within one community;
3. within a measurement-compatible subset of communities;
4. federation-wide comparable;
5. explicit shared federation floor.

Do not automatically promote a local success metric into a movement-wide benchmark.

## B39-L07 — Shared-measurement coalitions can be narrower than the federation

**Status:** PRACTICAL / KNOWN

Not every community needs to be comparable on every dimension.

Overlapping research subsets can exist for:
- ecological accounting;
- child development;
- labor accounting;
- relationship/wellbeing research;
- governance incidents;
- fission/reproduction.

A community may participate in several measurement coalitions while retaining value pluralism elsewhere.

## B39-L08 — Report success relative to the community's own declared goals as a separate output

**Status:** PRACTICAL / KNOWN

Intentional-community scholarship already proposes incorporating fulfillment of a community's own intentions rather than using longevity alone.

That should be one output, not the only one.

Report separately:
- own-goal attainment;
- comparable factual outcomes;
- federation-floor outcomes;
- tradeoffs and externalities;
- affected-person/leaver/child outcomes.

A community can achieve its chosen mission while still performing poorly on another dimension; do not let one score erase the other.

## B39-L09 — Satisfaction is an outcome, not a universal welfare function

**Status:** PRACTICAL / KNOWN

Do not solve value pluralism simply by ranking member satisfaction.

Satisfaction is itself affected by:
- expectations;
- adaptation;
- local meanings;
- self-selection;
- ambition level;
- measurement differences;
- whose voices remain in the community.

Use it as one outcome among others.

## B39-L10 — Show recommendation sensitivity to community values

**Status:** PRACTICAL / KNOWN

For a candidate practice, show:
- recommendation stable under most/all declared community value profiles;
- recommendation flips when one tradeoff changes;
- recommendation only fits communities with a specific objective/floor;
- no justified recommendation because the outcome tradeoff is unresolved.

This makes the normative hinge visible.

## B39-L11 — Label value-setting statements separately from empirical statements

**Status:** FOUNDATIONAL GOVERNANCE CONTROL

Research outputs should mark whether a claim is:
- empirical estimate;
- measurement assumption;
- transportability judgment;
- community-specific value application;
- federation constitutional norm;
- analyst/model choice;
- unresolved value dispute.

This prevents analytical expertise from quietly turning into political authority.

## B39-L12 — The federation research trilemma

**Status:** DESIGN HEURISTIC / KNOWN SOURCE THEORY

A federation cannot simply assume it simultaneously has:

1. unrestricted genuine value pluralism;
2. one complete federation-wide ranking of practices/communities;
3. a normatively neutral research center.

A complete ranking under plural values requires some aggregation/weighting rule. The rule may be legitimate, but it is a normative institution and should be treated as such.

---

# Proposed research-center default

Unless the federation explicitly chooses otherwise:

1. collect comparable factual/outcome vectors;
2. run C022 measurement-comparability checks;
3. estimate causal/contextual relationships;
4. preserve severe/non-compensable outcomes separately;
5. report Pareto/dominance/tradeoff structure;
6. provide community-conditional recommendations;
7. report federation-floor constraints only where explicitly adopted;
8. do **not** publish a universal scalar community ranking.

---

# Why this matters

The federation-level research center does **not** fail merely because values differ.

What fails is the stronger ambition that science can neutrally tell every community which life is best.

The center remains extremely useful as:
- causal mapper;
- measurement/calibration infrastructure;
- archive of failures and outcomes;
- replication/transportability evaluator;
- tradeoff calculator;
- community-specific decision support;
- federation-floor monitor where constitutionally authorized.

It should not become the unacknowledged source of movement-wide values.
