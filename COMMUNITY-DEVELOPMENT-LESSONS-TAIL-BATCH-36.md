# Community Development Lessons — Creative Tail Batch 36

Updated: 2026-08-15  
Status: append-only practical supplement; no new originality survivor

## Main lesson — Test quorum **safety** as well as quorum liveness
**Status:** PRACTICAL / KNOWN + C015 EXTENSION

A constitution can make every individual decision body valid yet still allow two simultaneously valid paths to authorize incompatible irreversible actions.

For high-consequence decision families, test both:

- **liveness:** some valid path remains available under recusals, vacancies, absences and conflicts;
- **safety/consistency:** two valid paths cannot leave incompatible authoritative states live at the same time without a predefined reconciliation rule.

---

## B36-L01 — Enumerate valid coalitions after recusal and vacancy
**Status:** PRACTICAL / KNOWN

For each relevant body:

1. list eligible members;
2. apply realistic conflict/recusal cases;
3. apply vacancies/absences/substitutions;
4. enumerate which groups still constitute valid quorums;
5. record which powers each quorum can exercise.

For small boards/committees this can be exhaustive rather than intuitive.

---

## B36-L02 — Look for pairs of incompatible valid orders
**Status:** PRACTICAL / KNOWN

Examples can include:

- local body expels while another body restores membership;
- one body freezes funds while another authorizes disbursement;
- one body restricts child/access role while another reinstates it;
- emergency authority and normal authority issue contradictory orders;
- two committees both claim final jurisdiction over the same property or records.

The question is not whether either body is illegitimate. It is whether the combined constitution defines one coherent state.

---

## B36-L03 — Predefine stay, precedence and supersession rules
**Status:** PRACTICAL / KNOWN

If two stages/bodies can affect the same high-consequence state, specify:

- whether appeal automatically stays the original decision;
- which temporary safety measures remain during appeal;
- when an appellate order supersedes a local order;
- when emergency authority expires;
- who resolves coequal jurisdiction conflict;
- which order/version is current.

Do not force the parties to discover these semantics during the conflict itself.

---

## B36-L04 — Do not copy distributed-system quorum intersection literally into appeals
**Status:** PRACTICAL / NEGATIVE TRANSFER

Distributed systems often require decision quorums to intersect to preserve consistency.

An independent appeal body may need the opposite personnel property: implicated original decision makers should be absent or recused.

So preserve the **consistency goal**, not the literal implementation.

Across original decision and independent appeal, consistency should usually come from explicit state transition/precedence rules rather than shared conflicted personnel.

---

## B36-L05 — Version high-consequence orders/case states
**Status:** PRACTICAL / KNOWN

Where multiple authorities can modify one case/state, record enough lineage to answer:

- what prior order this modifies?;
- what is superseded?;
- what remains active?;
- what is temporary?;
- which appeal/review is pending?;
- who had authority for this transition?

This is a governance record-control aid, not a substitute for substantive fairness.

---

## B36-L06 — Distinguish emergency temporary authority from final decision authority
**Status:** EVIDENCE-SUPPORTED + PRACTICAL

A body/person may need power to create immediate temporary safety without having authority to make the final finding/sanction.

Write the handoff explicitly:

`temporary protection → independent/ordinary review → final/appealable state`

and state what happens if ordinary review is delayed or unavailable.

---

## B36-L07 — Give coequal bodies a conflict route instead of a race to act
**Status:** PRACTICAL / KNOWN

If two committees/communities/federation bodies have legitimate overlapping authority, define what happens when they disagree before either takes an irreversible step.

Possible forms include:

- designated neutral/federation resolver;
- joint session;
- temporary status quo;
- narrow emergency exception;
- predefined subject-matter priority.

`Whichever acts first` should not become the accidental constitution.

---

## B36-L08 — Rerun coalition tests after amendments and personnel-structure changes
**Status:** PRACTICAL / C012-C015 EXTENSION

A small rule change can alter:

- quorum size;
- who recuses;
- who can appoint replacements;
- appeal membership;
- emergency authority;
- override threshold.

Rerun the safety/liveness scenarios rather than reviewing the changed clause in isolation.

---

# Batch 36 disposition

No new Creative Tail survivor. Distributed quorum systems own the intersection theory, while community/cooperative/legal governance already contains quorum, recusal, appeal and jurisdiction rules.

The status-quo improvement is:

> **A valid quorum is not enough. Verify that the complete set of valid quorums and appeal/emergency paths cannot create contradictory authoritative states—and preserve appeal independence while doing it.**
