# Main lesson — Do not let `best practice` diffusion eliminate all safe institutional alternatives

**Status:** PRACTICAL / KNOWN + RESEARCH ARCHITECTURE

Organizational-learning and policy-experimentation literature already owns the exploration–exploitation mechanism: rapid diffusion of a currently superior practice can improve short-run performance while reducing diversity needed for future discovery.

The communal research-center application is still important.

A federation research center should distinguish:
- **safety/rights floors**, which can legitimately rule some practices out;
- **optimization recommendations above the floor**, where uncertainty/context/value pluralism can justify preserving multiple voluntary variants.

---

## B40-L01 — `Best observed` is not `finished`

Whenever recommending a practice, record:
- evidence strength;
- context/transportability;
- scaling regime;
- value profile;
- uncertainty;
- reversibility;
- whether materially different safe alternatives remain observable.

## B40-L02 — Track institutional diversity as a research variable

Periodically record materially distinct functional variants for:
- governance;
- labor accounting;
- admissions;
- conflict/separation;
- childcare/family architecture;
- land/property/economic systems;
- care/support;
- fission/reproduction.

Do not count renamed but functionally identical practices as diversity.

## B40-L03 — Review before eliminating the last materially different safe variant

Before a recommendation/funding regime effectively extinguishes the last substantial admissible alternative, ask:

1. what information is lost if this variant disappears?;
2. is its inferiority robust across relevant community values and contexts?;
3. is it unsafe, or merely lower-performing on current weighted outcomes?;
4. can a voluntary small experimental lane preserve it without imposing costs on unwilling residents?;
5. how reversible is its disappearance?;
6. is implementation history preserved well enough to reconstruct later?;
7. what evidence would justify permanent abandonment?

This is an adaptive-learning review, not a novelty claim.

## B40-L04 — Preserve voluntary exploration, not mandatory human experimentation

Possible exploration mechanisms:
- communities already choosing different safe variants;
- voluntary daughter/new communities trying alternatives;
- shadow/simulation trials where real variation would be unethical;
- detailed preservation of retired architectures.

Do not assign people to inferior/unwanted conditions merely to improve the research design.

## B40-L05 — Diffusion speed should follow evidence quality and reversibility

Faster diffusion is more defensible when:
- effects are large/robust;
- harm risk is low;
- context dependence is small;
- measurement is comparable;
- value sensitivity is low;
- reversal is easy.

Partial/slow diffusion is more defensible when those conditions fail.

## B40-L06 — Measurement standardization is not institutional standardization

A federation can standardize:
- definitions;
- data schema;
- outcome follow-up;
- reporting formats;

without requiring every community to use the same governance/labor/family/economic system.

Common measurement can improve learning while institutional variation remains.

## B40-L07 — Recommendation-induced adoption is part of the causal environment

Once the center recommends X:
- better-resourced communities may adopt first;
- training/funding may follow adopters;
- non-adopters may become selected/stigmatized;
- later outcome differences partly reflect support/adoption selection.

Record recommendation timing, adoption timing, training/funding, and support exposure.

## B40-L08 — Preserve why abandoned variants were abandoned

Connect to C018 replicated-governance tombstones.

For a retired variant record whether it was abandoned because of:
- safety/rights failure;
- poor average outcome;
- value mismatch;
- cost;
- context change;
- political choice;
- evidence later shown weak.

This allows future researchers to distinguish `do not resurrect this danger` from `this lost an optimization contest under one past context`.

## B40-L09 — Do not let federation values erase legitimate minority-value communities

Connect to Batch 39.

A community design can score lower under federation-default weights yet be preferable under a legitimate local value profile. If it clears shared federation floors, lower federation-average utility is not itself a reason to eliminate it.

## B40-L10 — Convergence needs a cause-of-convergence audit

Movement similarity can reflect:
- strong evidence;
- imitation/herding;
- coercive federation rules;
- grant/funding selection;
- common training;
- market pressure;
- shared values;
- disappearance of alternative niches.

Record why convergence happened before treating it as evidence that the converged design is naturally superior.

## B40-L11 — Separate research recommendation from governance requirement

Label distinctly:
- `evidence favors X`;
- `federation constitution requires X`;
- `federation funds X`;
- `research center currently recommends X`;
- `other variants are prohibited`.

Research authority should not silently become regulatory authority.

## B40-L12 — Preserve safe option diversity as research infrastructure

Where differences are ethically acceptable and voluntarily chosen, institutional diversity itself is part of the movement's ability to learn.

This does not imply diversity for its own sake or tolerance of known severe harms. It means optimization uncertainty should not automatically be converted into movement monoculture.

# Combined Batch 39–40 research-center rule

A plural federation research center should:

1. test measurement comparability;
2. separate empirical effects from value aggregation;
3. produce causal/tradeoff maps rather than default universal rankings;
4. standardize data more readily than institutions;
5. accelerate diffusion when evidence is robust and low-risk;
6. preserve some voluntary safe institutional diversity while uncertainty and value sensitivity remain material;
7. distinguish constitutional floors from optimization experiments.
