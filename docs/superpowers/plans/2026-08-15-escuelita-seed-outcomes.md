# Escuelita seed-outcomes research plan

Date: 2026-08-15 (Africa/Dakar)
Status: completed bounded research unit
Scope: P0 research only; no article prose revised

## Owner question

Did the seeds carried outward from the 2013-2014 Zapatista Escuelita produce durable organizations, communities, governance practices, or measurable improvements outside Zapatista territory?

## Why this is a separate unit

The autonomy/legal-pluralism correction established that Zapatismo deliberately teaches and diffuses communal practice. It did not establish what happened to Escuelita participants afterward. Inspiration, continuing solidarity, practice transfer, organization creation, durable communal replication, and human outcomes are different endpoints.

This unit tests the outward-diffusion claim without reopening the completed *Communal Societies* or standalone corpora.

## Seed lineage ladder

A candidate is classified at the highest level directly supported by evidence:

1. **Encounter:** a person attended or used Escuelita materials.
2. **Continued relation:** alumni remained identifiable and acted together afterward.
3. **Practice transfer:** a participant reports applying a Zapatista principle in later work.
4. **New organization:** a named project was created after the Escuelita with a documented participant or alumni role.
5. **Durable communal institution:** the project persists and organizes shared life or governance, not only events or solidarity.
6. **Human outcomes:** safety, autonomy, child wellbeing, material viability, voluntary retention, or another defined outcome is measured over time.

“Sprouted” requires at least level 2. A strong claim of communal replication requires levels 4 and 5. A claim of success in the owner's sense requires level 6.

## Attribution controls

- Do not infer Escuelita descent from Zapatista vocabulary, affinity, or a post-2013 date.
- Separate the 2013-2014 Escuelita from the broader Zapatista movement, the Sixth Declaration, earlier encuentros, solidarity work, and later CompArte, conCiencias, women's encuentros, and Journey for Life.
- Treat organizations documented before 2013 as preexisting soil or possible grafts, not Escuelita seedlings.
- Require an explicit participant, alumni, founder, or organizational statement for a direct lineage claim.
- Preserve mixed lineage where Escuelita alumni acted with older collectives.
- Treat a bounded null as a report on this search, not proof that no descendant exists.

## Search lanes

1. Official EZLN descriptions of scale, method, and intended outward transmission.
2. Officially reposted alumni statements, actions, fundraisers, marches, and events from 2014 onward.
3. Named participant follow-up and first-person accounts.
4. Scholarly work on Zapatista seed pedagogics and transnational diffusion.
5. Candidate organizations with post-2013 activity and explicit alumni participation.
6. Pre-2013 transnational Zapatista organizations and pedagogies as attribution controls.
7. Searches for longitudinal cohorts, registries, residential communities, autonomous governance descendants, and measured human outcomes.
